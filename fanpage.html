The Chocolatier

Willy Wonka

Magician · Inventor · World's Most Ambitious Chocolatier

Long before he owned a single storefront, Willy Wonka stepped off a boat in a chocolate-obsessed city with twelve silver sovereigns, one suitcase, and a head full of recipes nobody had ever tasted. He couldn't read a contract to save his life — but he could make a chocolate bar do things chocolate had no business doing.

Raised on his mother's belief that good things never come to those who wait, he spent his early years learning sleight of hand and studying cacao in equal measure. By the time he opened his shop, he wasn't just selling candy — he was trying to prove that kindness and imagination could still beat a room full of greedy men in suits.

Hover Chocs

A marvelous morsel, an incredible edible, the Hoverchoc is an exotic combination of purest Wonka chocolate, marshmallow (from the mallow-marshes of Peru), caramel (salted with the bitter-sweet tears of a Russian Clown) and cherry (cherry-picked by the pick of the cherry pickers in the Imperial Gardens of Japan.) A chocolate that lifts you clean off your feet — best eaten sitting down.

Giraffe Milk Macaroon

Made with the distilled milk of a giraffe, this macaroon will restore your zeal and make you feel the tallest in the room. Say farewell to feeling small, and frightened of it all. With Wonka’s Giraffe Milk Macaroon, you’ll feel ten feet tall!

Big Night Out

A single chocolate that mimics a night on the town. The outer layer is champagne truffle. Then comes white wine, followed by red. That’s when the singing and dancing starts. Next is a layer of whisky fudge (which may prompt deep emotions and reckless behavior), followed by some old port which prompts a deep lasting sleep.

Hair Repair Eclair

Lost your hair? Can’t think quite where? Feeling fairly bare up there? Don’t despair! Wonka’s prepared! Behold his hair repair eclair. Made from ground vanilla, from the mountains of Manila, it includes a single drop of yeti sweat which will prompt full, thick growth. Try mixing it with chameleon juice for even more colorful results!
